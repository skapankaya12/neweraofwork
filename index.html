Case studies

Logistics: invoice-to-PO matching, 3 days a week to automatic

A 60-person logistics company was spending three days a week manually matching freight invoices to purchase orders across four systems. We built a custom AI agent that matches automatically, flags exceptions for human review, and sends a daily exceptions report. Live in six weeks.

B2B SaaS: inbound lead triage, 6–9 hours to under 4 minutes

An eight-person sales team was manually reading, categorizing and routing every inbound lead. We built an AI workflow that classifies leads in real time, scores them, and routes them to the right rep with a pre-drafted first response. Response time dropped from 6–9 hours to under four minutes.

Professional services: client reporting, 4–5 hours to 20 minutes

A professional services firm was building monthly client reports by hand, pulling data from three tools and writing commentary. We built a reporting agent that pulls the data, writes the commentary, and assembles a formatted draft for partner review. The firm took on 40% more clients without adding headcount.
