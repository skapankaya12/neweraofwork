## Selected projects

### Logistics: invoice-to-PO matching across 4 systems
A 60-person logistics firm was spending **3 days a week** manually matching invoices to purchase orders across four disconnected systems. We built an agent that matches automatically, flags only the exceptions for a human, and sends a daily report.
**Result:** live in **6 weeks**; three days of weekly manual work became a daily 20-minute exception review.

### B2B SaaS: inbound sales triage
An 8-person sales team was manually triaging inbound leads, with a **6 to 9 hour** response time. We built a system that classifies, scores, and routes every lead in real time, with a pre-drafted first response ready to send. Edge cases escalate to a queue.
**Result:** response time dropped from 6 to 9 hours down to **under 4 minutes**.

### Professional services: monthly client reporting
A firm was spending **4 to 5 hours** per monthly client report, by hand. We built a reporting agent that pulls the data, writes the commentary in the firm's voice, and assembles a review-ready draft for partner sign-off.
**Result:** each report dropped to **about 20 minutes**; the firm took on **40% more clients** with no new headcount.

## What we build (and ship in weeks)

### Custom company GPT or Claude assistant
A private assistant trained on your own playbooks, policies, product docs, and past work. Your team asks it questions in plain language and gets accurate answers grounded in your material, not the open internet.
*Example:* a sales assistant that drafts proposals in your company's voice, pulls the right pricing, and answers product questions instantly, so a new rep performs like a senior one in week one.

### Custom Claude automations
End-to-end automations that run a repetitive workflow from trigger to finished output, no one touching it in between.
*Example:* every new contract that lands in your inbox gets read, key terms extracted and checked against your standard terms, and a summary plus flag list posted to the right channel before anyone opens the file. Or a weekly automation that pulls numbers from your tools, writes the commentary, and drops a finished draft report in your folder every Monday morning.

### Custom workspaces per department and per person
We set up each team and each person with what their specific job needs: the right Claude skills, projects, and saved instructions for recurring tasks, the documentation and reference material wired in, and the small automations that kill the daily copy-paste.
*Example:* finance gets a reconciliation skill and a month-end checklist project; support gets a ticket-drafting workspace loaded with your tone and macros; ops gets an automation that turns messy email requests into structured tasks. Each department gets a workspace built for how it actually works.

### The building blocks underneath
Everything above is assembled from a few core capabilities we ship fast: **document & data processing** (extract from PDFs, emails, and forms; classify, validate, flag exceptions), **inbound triage & routing** (score and route leads, tickets, or emails in real time with pre-drafted replies), **automated reporting** (pull from your sources, write in your voice, hand over a draft), **knowledge agents** (instant answers over your own docs and policies), and **workflow glue** (automations that kill the copy-paste between tools).
